GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
before you enter.

Is Interview Dungeon a full game yet?+

Not yet. The project is in the design stage. You can play a shortened Floor III demo from the Google SWE Intern path today.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. The prototype runs in your browser, including its Python coding exercises.

Where is my progress saved?+

Demo progress is stored in your browser only. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is the project affiliated with Google?+

No. Company names describe the interview target a path prepares for. Interview Dungeon is not affiliated with those companies.
